Mohammad Kazemi

Research Associate in Edge AI · Imperial College London

I am a Research Associate in Edge AI in the Department of Computing at Imperial College London, where I work with Professor Julie McCann in the Adaptive Emergent Systems Engineering (AESE) group.

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, information theory, and wireless communications. I study how mathematical structure can make learning and communication systems more reliable and resource-efficient. Current directions include coding-theoretic robustness, calibration and verification of neural models, compact neural representations, over-the-air computation for federated learning, and unsourced random access. I also work on the fundamental limits of channels with insertion and deletion errors.

Previously, I was a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow in Imperial’s Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering and a postdoctoral researcher at Bilkent University. I received my PhD in Electrical Engineering from Amirkabir University of Technology in 2017.

I also advise Xymphonia AI on reliable and governable AI systems. I am an IEEE Senior Member, a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy, and an editor for IEEE Communications Letters in the Machine Learning area.
